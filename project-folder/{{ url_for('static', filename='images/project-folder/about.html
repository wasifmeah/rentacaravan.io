Why I Built This

I created RentMyCaravan.io to make it easier for people to find, list, and rent caravans without relying on big, expensive rental platforms. The idea came from seeing how many people own caravans that sit unused for most of the year, while others are searching for affordable, flexible ways to explore the countryside or travel with family. This platform connects those two groups. It’s meant to empower everyday people to earn from what they already own, while giving others a budget-friendly alternative to traditional rentals or hotels. What really drove me to start this project was the lack of user-friendly, community-centered caravan rental sites. Existing options were either too corporate, too expensive, or too clunky. I wanted something simpler — something that felt more like eBay or Facebook Marketplace but made specifically for caravans. I imagined a place where you could log in, post a listing with a few photos, and be up and running in minutes. That’s the kind of tool I’ve tried to build. More than just a listing service, I want this platform to be useful for families, solo travelers, and adventurers alike. Whether you’re heading to a music festival, going off-grid for a few days, or just needing an extra room at the beach, caravans are a versatile solution. My hope is that this site makes that option more accessible and helps create a little more freedom in people’s travel plans — all while supporting regular people instead of big businesses. This isn’t just a project for me. It’s something I believe can genuinely help people save money, earn a bit extra, and experience more of the world on their terms.

About Us

We’re Wasif Meah and Shakil Miah, two developers currently studying at Cardiff Metropolitan University. We met during our first year of university while working on a group assignment, and quickly realised we shared a similar passion for building practical, user-focused technology. Our friendship grew through late-night coding sessions, shared coursework stress, and a mutual goal of wanting to create something meaningful before we graduate. RentMyCaravan.io was born from that mindset. We were chatting one day about how many people we knew had caravans just sitting on driveways or in storage, and how hard it was to find a decent, straightforward platform for renting them out. That’s when the idea struck — why not build one ourselves? We knew it would be a challenge, but also a great opportunity to apply the skills we’ve been learning in uni to a real-world project. Working together, we split the responsibilities across front-end and back-end, designed the flow of the site, and tried to make something clean, functional, and actually useful for people. This project means a lot to us because it's not just coursework — it's a chance to solve a real problem, and to prove to ourselves that we can turn an idea into a live product. We hope that the site continues to grow and becomes a helpful tool for both caravan owners and renters across the UK.
